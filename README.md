1Factory is a leading provider of quality management software solutions for manufacturing companies. The platform helps businesses streamline their operations, improve efficiency, and ensure product quality at every stage of the production process. Features include real-time monitoring, automated data collection, advanced analytics, and integration with ERP and PLM systems via a REST API.

**Areas:** Analytics · Data Collection · Manufacturing · Monitoring · Quality

### APIs (7)

- [1Factory Customers API](https://apis.io/apis/1factory/1factory-customers-api/) — Data shared with your organization by your customers.
- [1Factory Manufacturing API](https://apis.io/apis/1factory/1factory-manufacturing-api/) — Data related to parts manufactured by your organization.
- [1Factory Part Master API](https://apis.io/apis/1factory/1factory-part-master-api/) — Part Master defines the part numbers, revisions and other information for parts referenced in Plans, Inspections, FAIs etc.
- [1Factory QMS API](https://apis.io/apis/1factory/1factory-qms-api/) — Data related to Quality
- [1Factory Receiving API](https://apis.io/apis/1factory/1factory-receiving-api/) — Data related to parts received by your organization.
- [1Factory Suppliers API](https://apis.io/apis/1factory/1factory-suppliers-api/) — Data shared with your organization by your suppliers, or details about your suppliers.
- [1Factory Work Orders API](https://apis.io/apis/1factory/1factory-work-orders-api/) — Work Orders define a list of current work order primary & secondary identifiers that may be used as identifiers on Inspections & FAIs.

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for 1Factory: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
